## V5.2 security rules

1. WordPress Administrator remains a technical super-admin and is outside the business role matrix.
2. Managing Partner has firm-wide Legal Management access.
3. Partner has internal Legal Management access but must be scoped to assigned/authorized matters before production.
4. Lawyer and Paralegal can operate legal workflow modules but cannot manage Retainer billing records.
5. Finance is limited to client read access and Retainer operations.
6. Client Admin and Client User do not receive direct WordPress CPT capabilities. Their access will be implemented through the Client Portal and matter/client authorization layer.
7. REST endpoints for Legal Management objects require authentication and an internal Legal Management role. Client portal API routes will be separate and explicitly scoped.
8. Object-level Client/Matter isolation remains a V5.5/V5.12 hardening requirement; V5.2 deliberately blocks client roles from internal CPT access rather than exposing broad CPT capabilities.

## QA boundary

V5.2 establishes the role/capability baseline. It is not a claim that matter-level authorization, private document delivery, audit logging, or client isolation is complete. Those controls must be completed before production use with confidential legal records.
